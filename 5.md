### Why not images on Vercel?

Vercel is excellent for your **application**, but object storage is a better place for a growing media library.

For example, if you eventually have:

```text
500 contractor profiles
× 5 images
= 2,500 images

+ blog images
+ HVAC product images
+ logos
+ diagrams
```

I'd rather have those in R2.

Your database only stores something like:

```text
contractor
    name: "ABC HVAC"
    logo: "https://cdn.../abc-hvac.webp"
    images: [...]
```

while the actual files live in R2.

## One important correction

**Payload CMS doesn't mean the CMS data is stored in Vercel.**

Payload is the application/admin layer.

Your actual data lives in PostgreSQL:

```text
Payload
   ↓
Neon
   ↓
PostgreSQL
```

So your 1,400+ PSEO combinations aren't sitting inside your Vercel deployment.

That's exactly what you want.
