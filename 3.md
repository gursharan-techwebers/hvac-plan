## Bottom line

Your current pages are **good enough as a product prototype but not where I'd stop for a 1,400-page production launch.**

The design and calculator are already strong.

The content needs to move from:

> **"same article with variables replaced"**

to:

> **"same excellent framework containing different data, explanations, comparisons, and evidence appropriate to each page."**

And I would absolutely **not try to make every paragraph unique**. That would create a huge amount of low-value generated prose.

Instead, make the **data, insights, calculations, sources, comparisons, and genuinely location-specific information unique where it matters**.

If you implement that correctly, the 1,400 pages start looking much more like a **structured HVAC cost database/tool** than 1,400 SEO landing pages—which is exactly the direction I'd take HVAC Cost Buddy.
